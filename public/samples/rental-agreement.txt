RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 April 2026 between Mr. Ramesh Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. Anita Verma (the "Tenant").

Landlord: Mr. Ramesh Sharma (ramesh.sharma@example.com, +91 98765 43210)
Tenant: Ms. Anita Verma

1. Property. The Landlord lets the flat at 402, Lotus Heights, Jaipur (the "Premises") to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. Term. This agreement is for a period of eleven (11) months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027. It shall renew automatically for a further period of eleven months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the term.

3. Rent. The monthly rent is Rs. 18,000, payable on or before the 5th day of each month. The Landlord may increase the rent by up to 10% at the end of each year, and by any further amount at his sole discretion on renewal.

4. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 54,000 (three months' rent). The Landlord may deduct any and all amounts he considers necessary for damages, and the balance will be refunded after the Tenant vacates.

5. Late Payment. If rent is not paid by the 5th of the month, the Tenant shall pay a late charge of 3% per month on the overdue amount.

6. Lock-in Period. There is a lock-in period of six (6) months. If the Tenant vacates during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall forfeit the security deposit and pay rent for the remaining lock-in period.

7. Repairs and Maintenance. The Tenant shall bear all repairs and maintenance of the Premises, including structural repairs.

8. Inspection. The Landlord or his agents may enter and inspect the Premises at any time without prior notice.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice. The Landlord may terminate this agreement immediately without notice if the Tenant breaches any term.

10. Default. If the Tenant defaults, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises and the Tenant shall vacate the Premises forthwith.

11. Indemnity. The Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord against any and all claims arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises.

12. Disputes. All disputes shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Landlord in Jaipur. The courts in Jaipur shall have exclusive jurisdiction. This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

13. Entire Agreement. This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and promises.
