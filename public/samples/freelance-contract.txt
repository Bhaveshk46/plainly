INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on 10 May 2026 between Northwind Digital Pvt. Ltd. (the "Client") and Priya Nair (the "Contractor").

1. Services. The Contractor shall redesign the Client's website and deliver the deliverables set out in the Statement of Work, and shall make unlimited revisions until the Client is satisfied.

2. Fees and Payment. The Client shall pay the Contractor a fixed fee of Rs. 1,20,000. Each invoice is payable within 60 days of receipt. Late payment by the Client shall not attract interest.

3. Intellectual Property. All work product, inventions and deliverables, whether or not created during working hours, shall be the exclusive property of the Client. The Contractor hereby assigns all right, title and interest in the work product to the Client from the moment of creation, including all moral rights, and irrespective of payment.

4. Exclusivity. During the term the Contractor shall not provide services to any other client.

5. Non-Competition. For a period of 24 months after termination, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, work for any competitor of the Client anywhere in India.

6. Confidentiality. The Contractor shall keep all Confidential Information confidential in perpetuity.

7. Indemnity. The Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client against any and all claims, losses and expenses arising from the Services.

8. Limitation of Liability. The Client's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed Rs. 10,000. In no event shall the Client be liable for indirect or consequential loss.

9. Termination. The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice or cause. The Contractor may terminate only by giving ninety (90) days' written notice.

10. Disputes. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Bengaluru. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

11. Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties.
