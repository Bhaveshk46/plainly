RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT)

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 April 2026 between Mr. Ramesh Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. Anita Verma (the "Tenant").

Landlord: Mr. Ramesh Sharma
Tenant: Ms. Anita Verma

1. Property. The Landlord lets the flat at 402, Lotus Heights, Jaipur (the "Premises") to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. Term. This agreement is for a period of eleven (11) months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027. Any renewal shall be by mutual written agreement.

3. Rent. The monthly rent is Rs. 19,500, payable on or before the 7th day of each month. The rent may be increased by up to 5% on renewal, with 60 days' prior written notice.

4. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 39,000 (two months' rent). The Landlord shall refund the deposit within 30 days after the Tenant vacates, after deducting only documented damages beyond normal wear and tear.

5. Late Payment. If rent is not paid by the 7th of the month, the Tenant shall pay a late charge of 1% per month on the overdue amount.

6. Lock-in Period. There is a lock-in period of eleven (11) months. If the Tenant vacates during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall forfeit the security deposit and pay rent for the remaining lock-in period.

7. Repairs and Maintenance. The Tenant shall bear minor day-to-day repairs. Structural repairs and major repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.

8. Inspection. The Landlord may inspect the Premises after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior notice, except in an emergency.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving sixty (60) days' written notice. The Landlord may terminate this agreement immediately without notice if the Tenant breaches any term.

10. Default. If the Tenant defaults and does not correct the default within 15 days of written notice, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises.

11. Indemnity. The Tenant agrees to indemnify the Landlord against claims arising from damage caused by the Tenant's negligence.

12. Disputes. Disputes shall first be discussed in good faith. Failing settlement, the courts in Jaipur shall have jurisdiction. This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

13. Entire Agreement. This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and promises.
